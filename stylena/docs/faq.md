# FAQ

**Does the theme contact other websites?**
Not on public pages by default. Optional, admin-initiated only: the demo media download, Google Fonts (off by default), and Google Places reviews. Google Places is off by default and only contacts Google after you tick its opt-in in the Customizer and enter your own key.

**Does it work without Stylena Core?**
The theme loads, but content-type sections are empty. Install Stylena Core for the full layout.

**Is the sample content included in the download?**
The text and layout are. The AI-generated sample photos and hero video are downloaded from the demo server during import; they are not in the theme and must be replaced with your own before launch.

**Which fonts are used?**
Montserrat by default; Exo and Aboreto are also bundled. All bundled fonts are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License.

**Can I translate it?**
Yes. Text domain `stylena`, template at `languages/stylena.pot`, RTL stylesheet included.

**Can I use a child theme?**
Yes, recommended for code changes.
